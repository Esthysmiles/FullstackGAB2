About the Project

A public record kept by the poeple who live here

Anyone in OgoniLand can report an oil spill, a gas flare, polluted water or dumped waste. Every report is public and HYPREP follow each report until it is resolved and closed.

How a report moves

1

You report it

Create an account with your phone number, login and report it - describing what you saw, where and when.

2

HYPREP reviews it

The HYPREP team checks and reviews every report. And visits the site when needed

3

Everyone can follow it up

The satus and HYPREPs remark are public until or even when it is closed.

What each status means

New Received, but not yet reviewed
Under Review This is currently being reviewed..
Resolved Reviewed and Resolved. The admin remark says how.
Not an incident Checked and it was not an incident

HYPREP staff: set statuses on the staff admin page
